# Deliverable and Diagram Requirements / 交付件与图表要求

## Default Deliverables / 默认交付件

Produce two files unless the user requests a different format:

1. Bilingual Word report (`.docx`): comprehensive, evidence-backed, with narrative, tables, and diagrams.
2. Bilingual PowerPoint deck (`.pptx`): executive-readable, visual-first, editable where practical.

The Word report is the evidence and analysis base. The PPT is the executive and workshop version.

## Bilingual Standard / 双语标准

- Chinese is the primary narrative language.
- English equivalents are required for section titles, chart titles, diagram labels, table headers, key findings, capability names, roadmap items, and customer discussion questions.
- Use concise bilingual labels on PPT slides rather than long parallel paragraphs.
- Keep original English/Portuguese/local regulatory names where useful, followed by Chinese explanation.

## Recommended Word Structure / Word 报告结构

1. Executive summary / 执行摘要.
2. Research scope, evidence map, and uncertainty notes / 研究范围、证据地图与不确定性说明.
3. Acronyms and name explanations / 缩略语与名称解释.
4. Customer profile and role definition / 客户概况与角色定位.
5. Market panorama and value chain / 市场全景与价值链.
6. Regulatory and industry context / 监管与行业背景.
7. Product-level market space and sizing method / 产品级市场空间与估算方法.
8. Competitor landscape, peer benchmark, and China analogues / 竞品格局、同业标杆与中国类比.
9. Business model and customer segments / 商业模式与客户分层.
10. Investors, ownership, and capital signals / 投资人、股权结构与资本信号.
11. Business development history, path, and goals / 业务发展历程、路径与目标.
12. Product portfolio, major customer examples, and capability map / 产品组合、主要客户与能力地图.
13. Operating process and regulatory process / 运营流程与监管流程.
14. API, data, and developer ecosystem / API、数据与开发者生态.
15. Product-by-product technical architecture / 分产品技术架构.
16. Overall platform architecture and deployment architecture / 整体平台架构与部署架构.
17. Cloud, resilience, workload placement, and continuity / 云、韧性、工作负载放置与连续性.
18. Security, compliance, privacy, audit, and risk / 安全、合规、隐私、审计与风险.
19. Opportunity roadmap and customer discussion questions / 机会路线图与客户讨论问题.
20. Sources, evidence notes, and appendix / 参考资料、证据说明与附录.

## Recommended PPT Structure / PPT 报告结构

Use a visual-first consulting deck. Recommended pages: cover, executive summary, evidence map, acronyms/roles, market panorama, regulatory value chain, product market space, competitors, peer benchmark and China analogues, customer positioning, customer segments, product portfolio, business path, operating process, regulatory process, API/data ecosystem, product architecture, deployment architecture, workload placement/roadmap, discussion questions.

## Required Diagram Types / 必备图表类型

Include diagrams where relevant:

- Market panorama or value-chain map.
- Product-market and competitor map.
- Peer benchmark matrix, including China analogues.
- Ecosystem and stakeholder map.
- Business capability map.
- End-to-end business process flow.
- Regulatory or industry process flow.
- API and solution capability map.
- Product-by-product technical architecture diagram.
- Overall layered technology architecture diagram.
- Deployment, hybrid cloud, or multi-cloud target architecture.
- Workload placement matrix.
- Opportunity roadmap.

## Diagram Quality Rules / 图表质量规则

- Arrows must point from upstream/source/trigger to downstream/target/outcome.
- For process diagrams, define start event, actors, handoffs, system boundaries, success path, exception path, and evidence/audit points.
- For architecture diagrams, separate customer-owned systems, partner systems, ecosystem infrastructure, regulators, and cloud provider components.
- For market diagrams, separate regulator, scheme, infrastructure operator, issuer, acquirer, PSP, processor, merchant, platform, and end user roles.
- Do not mix confirmed systems with inferred systems unless visually labeled.
- Use editable PowerPoint shapes where practical.
- Avoid dense text blocks, tiny labels, and overloaded slides.

## Final Quality Checklist / 最终质量检查

Confirm bilingual coverage, market/business/technology separation, correct section placement, readable diagrams, correct arrow direction, and source traceability for every major claim.
